# Why Adaptive Interfaces Matter

Adaptive interfaces change presentation based on the structure and meaning of content.

## The Problem

A fixed template can work well for one kind of information and poorly for another. A table-heavy dataset may need comparison, while a long article needs readable sections.

## A Different Approach

An AI planner can inspect the source and choose from a controlled component library.

- Structured data can become tables and charts.
- Key values can become metrics.
- Long-form prose can become headings and text.
- Chronological information can become a timeline.

## Guardrails

The AI should decide *what the interface should be*, not execute arbitrary frontend code.
